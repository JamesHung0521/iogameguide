War Brokers: Every Combat Role Ranked — Which One Actually Wins?

By iogameguide Editorial Team - Jamie Park•Updated: 2026-08-10•8 min read

🎮 Play War Brokers Now

The Big Question

Everyone argues about whether raw gun skill or vehicle mastery wins matches in War Brokers. I’ve spent dozens of hours in the 15-player lobbies, testing every angle, and let me tell you—the meta isn't what the forums claim. You can have the best aim in the browser, but if you don't understand how to integrate combined-arms combat, you will get rolled. I broke down the four primary combat roles to see which one actually dictates the flow of a match. From the slow-moving iron beasts to the high-flying choppers, I’m ranking them from absolute trash to the undisputed best. Buckle up, because my number one pick is going to make the tryhard helicopter pilots furious.

The Contenders

Tank Gunner — The Battering Ram

Effective Range: 300m | Mobility: Very Low | Anti-Vehicle: High | Anti-Infantry: Medium | Skill Floor: Low.
✓ The tank is the ultimate stalemate breaker. As the game mechanics note, it can shatter fortified positions that infantry simply cannot challenge. In Package Retrieval or Capture Point modes, your armor absorbs small-arms fire, creating a safe corridor for your team to push. The main gun’s explosive payload clears out entrenched enemies behind cover. For newer players, the skill floor is incredibly forgiving; you don't need twitch reflexes, just basic map awareness and timing. You are a walking frontline spawn point that dictates the enemy's movement.
✗ Your biggest counter is literally a single skilled sniper. Because your view is restricted and turret rotation is slow, a long-range headshot eliminates you instantly. In tight spaces like Dead End City, you become a trapped turtle, easily flanked by aggressive infantry. Furthermore, your low mobility means if the team needs to rapidly rotate across the massive 15-player maps, you will always be the last to arrive. You are highly predictable, and smart teams will simply route around you rather than engage head-on.
Slow, predictable, but absolutely essential for breaking defensive stalemates.

Helicopter Pilot — The Sky Terror

Effective Range: 400m | Mobility: Very High | Anti-Vehicle: Medium | Anti-Infantry: High | Skill Ceiling: High.
✓ Helicopters are the ultimate map-control tool. They allow you to quickly redeploy your team across massive maps, turning a lost point into a sudden flanking maneuver. From the air, you have unparalleled sightlines to spot enemy movements and provide close air support. In a game where 17 balanced weapons mean infantry fights are often drawn out, a heli breaks the stalemate in seconds. Mastering the flight physics gives you a massive psychological edge; the sound of rotors alone makes enemy infantry panic and break cover.
✗ The skill ceiling is brutally high. If you lack spatial awareness, you will crash into a building on takeoff, handing the enemy a free kill. Your armor is paper-thin; one well-placed rocket or sniper bullet turns your expensive ride into a fiery wreck. It requires constant micro-management of altitude and momentum, making it impossible to focus purely on aiming. In public lobbies with uncoordinated teams, you often end up flying solo into anti-air crossfires with zero infantry support to cover your blind spots.
The highest skill expression in the game, but a massive liability in untrained hands.

Infantry Sniper — The Operator Killer

Effective Range: 800m+ | Mobility: Low | Anti-Vehicle: High | Anti-Infantry: High | Skill Ceiling: Medium.
✓ This role is the hard counter to enemy vehicle reliance. The game explicitly allows a skilled sniper to eliminate vehicle operators from range, instantly turning a terrifying tank into an abandoned metal box. In defensive setups like Missile Launch, you can lock down long sightlines, forcing the enemy to waste time and resources just to cross open ground. With 17 balanced weapons, there are no pay-to-win damage drops; your success relies purely on bullet drop compensation and tracking. You control the engagement distance entirely.
✗ You are completely helpless in close-quarters combat. If an Assault Infantry player pushes your flank, you have zero escape options. Your low mobility makes you a liability during fast-paced objective rotations; by the time you walk across the map, the point is already lost. You also heavily depend on your team to create space. If your squad is getting pushed back, you’ll just get isolated and picked off. It’s a highly selfish role if you don't communicate your sightlines to the team.
The ultimate counter-pick that ruthlessly punishes enemy vehicle reliance.

Assault Infantry — The Objective Grinder

Effective Range: 100m | Mobility: High | Anti-Vehicle: Low | Anti-Infantry: High | Skill Floor: Medium.
✓ This is the foundational combat style. Utilizing spawn invulnerability to instantly reposition and grab key choke points is how you win the early game. Assault rifles provide the most consistent damage output in the 100-meter sweet spot where most infantry fights happen. You don't need to worry about vehicle physics or complex reload cycles; just pure aim, movement, and map knowledge. In modes like Team Deathmatch, your high mobility allows you to constantly flank and keep the enemy team off-balance.
✗ Let’s be brutally honest: you are entirely at the mercy of vehicles. Without a sniper or tank on your team, a single enemy helicopter or tank will wipe your entire squad. You have no tools to contest armor effectively. Your impact is also highly localized; you can win a gunfight, but you can't break a fortified objective alone. If the enemy team coordinates their combined-arms push, your individual gun skill means absolutely nothing against a synchronized armored assault.
The reliable backbone of every squad, but completely helpless against armor.

The Rankings

#1 Tank Gunner Rank 1 of 4
#2 Helicopter Pilot Rank 2 of 4
#3 Infantry Sniper Rank 3 of 4
#4 Assault Infantry Rank 4 of 4

Let's rank them from worst to best. 4. Assault Infantry: Overrated by beginners who think raw aim wins games. In War Brokers, pure gun skill is irrelevant if a tank rolls over your position. You are the foot soldiers, but you lack the agency to change the macro-game. 3. Infantry Sniper: A hidden gem for coordinated teams, but too fragile for chaotic public lobbies. You rely entirely on the enemy making the mistake of using vehicles. If they just play infantry, your impact drops significantly. 2. Helicopter Pilot: The ultimate flex. If you master it, you're a god. But I’m ranking it second because 90% of players just crash and die, making it a net negative for the team. It’s too high-risk for the average player. 1. Tank Gunner: The undisputed king. Low skill floor, massive impact. You control the pace of the match, absorb damage, and break objectives. You don't need to be a mechanical genius to drive a tank and win the game. It is the most consistent, game-winning role in the entire roster.

Who Should Pick What

If you're a beginner looking for impact
Go Tank Gunner
You don't need twitch reflexes to drive a tank. You provide immediate frontline pressure, absorb enemy fire, and break stalemates without the stress of managing flight physics or long-range bullet drop.
If you love fast-paced aggression
Go Assault Infantry
You get to focus purely on gunplay and movement. Use your high mobility to flank, utilize spawn invulnerability to push choke points, and dominate the close-quarters infantry fights.
If you play patiently and hate vehicles
Go Infantry Sniper
You get to sit back at 800 meters and dismantle the enemy team's armor. It’s highly rewarding to watch a tank crew panic and bail out after you snipe the driver.
If you're a tryhard with hours to spare
Go Helicopter Pilot
Mastering the flight model is the highest skill ceiling in the game. Once you can fly and shoot simultaneously, you become an untouchable god of the battlefield.

The Bottom Line

At the end of the day, War Brokers is about combined-arms combat, but the Tank Gunner is the undisputed best role. While helicopter pilots get all the flashy kills and snipers get the satisfaction of popping tires, the tank dictates the actual flow of the match. It forces the enemy to react to you, not the other way around. Stop trying to be a hero in an Assault Rifle, and stop crashing helicopters for the highlight reel. Grab a tank, push the objective, and watch your win rate skyrocket.
