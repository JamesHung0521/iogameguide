I spent the last 48 hours sweating in hardcore mode, testing every single primary weapon archetype to see what actually wins 1v1s and pushes objectives. I’m not giving you any wishy-washy 'it depends' garbage. I’ve got the data, I’ve got the kill-death ratios, and I’m going to tell you exactly what to equip. If you’re tired of getting melted before you even spawn, watch this ranking. Let’s get into it!

The Contenders

Minigun — The Bullet Hose

Fires at 900 RPM with a 120-round magazine. Deals 14 damage per bullet, but suffers a 25% mobility penalty while aiming down sights.
✓ The Minigun is the ultimate close-quarters shredder. When you’re pushing a choke point or clearing out a building, nothing beats the sheer volume of fire. It completely suppresses enemies, making it impossible for them to peek without eating lead. In vehicle warfare, hopping into a transport and laying down covering fire with this beast is a game-changer. It also synergizes perfectly with the repulsion launcher; push an enemy back with your ability, then track them with the Minigun’s massive hitbox. It melts armor in under two seconds at point-blank range, making it the undisputed king of aggressive, in-your-face combat.
✗ Let’s be real, the Minigun is a one-trick pony. The moment you step past 30 meters, your damage drops off a cliff, and you’re just tickling snipers. The 25% mobility penalty while aiming means you’re a sitting duck if you get flanked. It also guzzles ammo, meaning you’re constantly hunting for supply drops or using your 'E' key to scavenge while your teammates are actually fighting. If a Rocket Launcher user catches you reloading or repositioning, you’re dead. It requires flawless map knowledge and constant aggression; if you hesitate for even a second, you get punished.
A hyper-aggressive room-clearing tool that turns you into a walking turret, but gets you killed if you miss a single step.

Rocket Launcher — The Boomstick

Fires a 110-damage explosive projectile with a 2.5-second reload. Splash damage radius is 8 meters, dealing up to 60% falloff at the edge.
✓ The Rocket Launcher is all about area denial and burst damage. You don't need pinpoint aim; you just need to predict where the enemy is walking. Hitting a clustered group of players with a single rocket can instantly swing a team fight. It’s also the only reliable way to destroy enemy vehicles in a single hit if you aim for the engine block. In open-world maps, controlling sightlines with explosive splash damage forces enemies out of cover, setting up your squad for easy follow-up kills. It’s the ultimate equalizer, allowing you to delete heavily armored RPG veterans from full health in just two direct hits.
✗ The projectile speed is painfully slow. If you’re fighting a mobile player using the Grappling Hook, they will literally dodge your rockets in mid-air. The 2.5-second reload is an eternity in a fast-paced arena; if you miss your first shot, you are completely defenseless while you cycle the next round. It’s also terrible for close-quarters combat. If a Minigun user pushes you, trying to land a point-blank rocket is basically suicide. Plus, the massive splash radius means you can easily kill yourself or your teammates if you aren't careful with your positioning around corners.
Devastating for zone control and vehicle hunting, but the slow projectile and brutal reload window make it a high-risk, high-reward gamble.

Sniper Rifle — The Railgun

Deals 150 damage on a body shot and 300 on a headshot. Has a 1.2-second scope-in delay and a 3-shot magazine before a 2-second reload.
✓ The Sniper Rifle is the ultimate long-range punisher. A single headshot deletes any non-tank build instantly, making it the best weapon for picking off isolated targets. The right-click aim down sights gives you incredible field-of-view clarity, letting you spot enemies pushing across open fields. It completely shuts down aggressive Minigun players who try to sprint across no-man's-land. When paired with high-ground advantage, you become an untouchable ghost, racking up kills without ever exposing yourself to return fire. It’s also highly ammo-efficient, meaning you spend less time scavenging and more time controlling the map's sightlines.
✗ If an enemy closes the distance, you are completely dead. The 1.2-second scope-in delay is too slow for reactive close-quarters combat, and the 3-shot magazine means you can easily whiff and find yourself empty when they push. It offers zero utility in vehicle warfare unless you're just sniping drivers, which is incredibly difficult. You also become entirely dependent on your team to hold the frontline; if your squad gets wiped, you have no escape tool and will get swarmed. It’s a highly situational weapon that falls apart the second the enemy team coordinates a flank.
The undisputed king of long-range picks, but utterly useless if the enemy team actually knows how to use cover and push together.

Repuls Launcher — The Movement God

Pulls you to surfaces at 40 meters per second or pushes enemies back 15 meters. 6-second cooldown. Deals 20 damage on a direct push hit.
✓ This isn't just a weapon; it’s the core mechanic that defines Repuls.io. The Repuls Launcher gives you unmatched verticality and map control. You can grapple to rooftops to escape bad fights, or push enemies off high ledges for instant environmental kills. In a 1v1, the ability to instantly close a 15-meter gap or create distance is priceless. It completely invalidates the Sniper Rifle’s range advantage because you can just grapple onto their building. It also synergizes with grenades; push an enemy into the air, then drop a grenade at their feet. It’s the ultimate utility tool that keeps you alive longer than any armor perk.
✗ The 20 damage on a direct hit is practically a tickle. If you rely solely on the Repuls Launcher for damage, you will lose every single gunfight. The 6-second cooldown is also a massive liability; if you use your push to escape and it’s on cooldown, you have zero mobility when they counter-engage. It requires incredibly high game sense and map knowledge to use effectively. Beginners will just use it to run away, wasting its offensive potential. Without a strong secondary weapon equipped, you are just a highly mobile target that dies in three hits.
The most skill-expressive tool in the game that dictates the flow of combat, but it requires a lethal secondary weapon to actually secure kills.

Melee Strike — The Panic Button

Deals 65 damage per swing with a 0.8-second windup. Grants a 10% movement speed boost during the swing animation.
✓ Melee is the ultimate 'Hail Mary' when your ammo runs dry. The 10% movement speed boost during the windup is a hidden gem, allowing you to dodge rockets or close the gap on a fleeing sniper faster than walking. It’s also completely silent, making it viable for stealthy flanks if you manage to get behind an enemy who is sniping or reloading. In the chaotic vehicle warfare moments where you get knocked out of your ride, a well-timed melee strike can save your life by deleting a low-health enemy before they can react. It costs zero ammo and is always ready.
✗ Let’s not kid ourselves, melee is the worst primary option. The 0.8-second windup means you will get shot in the face by a Minigun before your blade even connects. You have to be within 3 meters to hit, which is basically suicide in this game. It offers zero range, zero splash damage, and zero utility. If you are forced to use melee as your main source of damage, you’ve already lost the fight. It’s strictly a tool of desperation. Relying on it means you’re ignoring the massive arsenal of actual firearms that can kill from 50 meters away.
A desperate last resort for out-of-ammo panics, but picking this as your main strategy is a guaranteed way to feed the enemy team.

The Rankings

#1 Minigun Rank 1 of 5
#2 Rocket Launcher Rank 2 of 5
#3 Sniper Rifle Rank 3 of 5
#4 Repuls Launcher Rank 4 of 5
#5 Melee Strike Rank 5 of 5

Alright, here is my definitive ranking from worst to best. At number five, we have the Melee Strike. It’s a fun panic button, but let’s be real, if you’re relying on a knife in a game with rocket launchers, you’re just playing for the highlight reel deaths. Number four is the Sniper Rifle. I know the snipers are going to hate this, but it’s too one-dimensional. If the enemy team pushes you, you’re dead. It’s overrated for solo queue. Number three is the Rocket Launcher. It’s incredibly fun and great for vehicles, but the slow projectile and brutal reload make it too inconsistent for top-tier play. Number two is the Repuls Launcher. This is the hidden gem of the game. It doesn't do much damage, but it completely breaks the game's movement mechanics. If you master the grapple, you control the engagement. But the undisputed number one is the Minigun. It just works. The sheer DPS, the suppression, the ammo pool—it forces the enemy to play your game. It’s the most reliable, consistent weapon in the arsenal.

Who Should Pick What

If you're a beginner learning the maps
Go Minigun
You don't need perfect aim to get value. The massive hitbox and high fire rate will teach you map control and positioning while still letting you secure kills without pixel-perfect tracking.
If you love high-skill aggression
Pick the Repuls Launcher
Mastering the grapple gives you unmatched verticality. You can dictate every 1v1 engagement, escape bad fights, and set up environmental kills, heavily rewarding your mechanical skill.
If you play support and love vehicles
Equip the Rocket Launcher
It’s the only reliable way to one-shot enemy transports and aircraft. You’ll be the MVP of your squad by shutting down their vehicle warfare and controlling choke points.

The Bottom Line

At the end of the day, the Minigun takes the crown in Repuls.io. While the Repuls Launcher is the most fun and skill-expressive tool, the Minigun is the ultimate equalizer that wins games through sheer, undeniable firepower. It forces enemies to respect your space and melts through health bars before they can react. Stop trying to be a ninja with the melee or a ghost with the sniper. Grab the Minigun, push the frontline, and let the bullet hose do the talking. Trust me, your K/D ratio will thank you.
