Arras.io: Every Tank Build Strategy Ranked — Which One Actually Wins?

🎮 Play Arras.io Now

The Big Question

Everyone in the Arras.io community argues about the ultimate way to dominate the leaderboard. Do you sit back and pick people off from across the map, or do you rush the Pentagon Nest and bully everyone out? I’ve spent hundreds of hours testing every stat allocation in Free For All and Maze, and let me tell you, most of you are playing the meta completely wrong. I took the five most popular tank build strategies, ran the numbers on damage, fire rate, range, and speed, and tested them against each other in the worst possible matchups. Some of these builds are absolute trash, while one is a completely broken hidden gem that will make you unstoppable. Here is my definitive ranking of the best tank build strategies in Arras.io, from worst to absolute best.

The Contenders

The Glass Cannon — The One-Shot Wonder

Maxes out Damage (100%) and Fire Rate (80%), but drops Health to a miserable 20% and Speed to 50%. Upgrade cost is brutal, requiring massive score grinding just to survive the first five minutes.
✓ When you actually land a hit, it hurts immensely. This build excels at bursting down high-tier tanks before they can react, especially when ambushing from behind a rock in Free For All. If you catch an unsuspecting player farming the Pentagon Nest, you can melt their entire health bar in just two seconds. It’s incredibly satisfying to watch a massive enemy tank completely evaporate because you prioritized raw damage over everything else, making you a deadly threat in open spaces.
✗ One single scratch and you’re completely dead. If you miss your shots, or if a fast Rusher catches you, you get instantly deleted from the server. You have zero escape velocity and absolutely no survivability. In a chaotic 4TDM match or a tight Maze, you will constantly get flanked and heavily punished for your lack of mobility. It’s a very high-risk build that relies entirely on the enemy making a mistake.
Fun for five minutes, but ultimately a frustrating way to play if you actually want to reach the top of the leaderboard.

The Bullet Hose — The Lead Wall

Pushes Fire Rate to 100% and Bullet Count to 80%, but severely limits Range to 40% and Damage to 50%. Speed sits at a mediocre 60%.
✓ This build is the undisputed king of close-quarters combat. When you’re fighting over the rocks in a standard FFA map, you can easily create an impenetrable wall of bullets that pushes enemies back. It’s amazing for clearing out the smaller shapes around the Pentagon Nest quickly, allowing you to farm levels safely while denying area control. If an enemy tries to push you, they have to chew through a massive amount of health-draining projectiles.
✗ You are completely useless at a distance. If a Sniper or a long-range build decides to poke you from outside your 40% range, you can’t do a single thing about it. You also get countered hard by high-speed builds that can simply dodge your slow, short-range spam and ram you. It’s far too one-dimensional for high-level play, leaving you incredibly vulnerable to anyone who knows how to maintain their distance.
Great for beginners who just want to mash the left mouse button, but too easily kited by anyone with basic game sense.

The Swarm — The Drone King

Maximizes Bullet Count to 100% and maintains a balanced Speed of 70% and FOV of 80%. Damage is moderate at 60%, but the sheer volume of projectiles makes up for it.
✓ This is the ultimate area-denial strategy. By surrounding your tank with a constant orbit of projectiles, you create a moving shield that makes it nearly impossible for enemies to get close. It shines in the Maze mode, where you can block off narrow corridors and force enemies to walk into your drone field. You don’t even need to aim perfectly; the autonomous nature of the swarm does the work for you while you focus on positioning and map awareness.
✗ The individual projectiles lack the raw punch to quickly burst down heavily armored tanks. If an enemy builds high Health and just pushes through your swarm, they can eventually reach you and deal massive damage. It also struggles heavily against long-range snipers who can easily pick you off before your swarm even reaches them, making it highly vulnerable to kiting strategies and long-range poke damage from safe distances across the entire map.
A brilliant, lazy-man’s dream for controlling space, but it falls apart against dedicated, high-damage burst builds.

The Rusher — The Unstoppable Force

Dumps everything into Speed (100%) and Health (100%), leaving Damage at 70% and Range at a pathetic 20%. Fire rate is barely 40%.
✓ Mobility is king in Arras.io, and this build completely weaponizes it. You can cross the entire FFA map in seconds, making you the ultimate ambush predator. By ramming enemies at max speed, you bypass their bullet shields entirely and deal massive body damage. This build completely ignores the meta because you’re not playing the shooting game; you’re playing the collision game. It’s incredibly effective at stealing Pentagon Nest kills and escaping bad situations in the Maze.
✗ You are entirely dependent on closing the gap quickly. If you run into a defensive build that kites you perfectly, you’ll just run face-first into their defenses and die. You also have terrible field of view and range, meaning you can easily get sniped before you even know where the shot came from, leaving you completely blind to long-range threats and highly vulnerable to surprise ambushes from hidden angles on the map.
The most chaotic and aggressive playstyle in the game, perfect for players who hate sitting still and love making their enemies panic.

The Sniper — The Map Hacker

Maxes out Range (100%) and Field of View (100%), with high Damage (80%) and moderate Fire Rate (60%). Speed is sacrificed to 40%.
✓ This is the undisputed king of map control. With 100% FOV and Range, you can see and shoot enemies from across the entire arena, completely bypassing the rock cover that ruins other builds. You can safely farm the Pentagon Nest from a distance, picking off anyone who tries to approach. In Maze mode, you can shoot through the randomized walls, turning the entire map into your personal shooting gallery. The data doesn't lie: controlling the engagement distance means you dictate every single fight.
✗ You are a complete sitting duck if someone gets in your face. Your 40% speed makes repositioning a nightmare, and if a Rusher or Bullet Hose gets within 30% range, you’re getting instantly deleted. You also have to be incredibly patient and have great map awareness, as you can’t just run away from bad situations, meaning one bad spawn can completely ruin your entire run and force a frustrating restart from Level 1.
The most technically demanding but mathematically overpowered build in the game; if you have the patience, nothing else comes close.

The Rankings

#1 The Glass Cannon Rank 1 of 5
#2 The Bullet Hose Rank 2 of 5
#3 The Swarm Rank 3 of 5
#4 The Rusher Rank 4 of 5
#5 The Sniper Rank 5 of 5

Here is my definitive ranking from worst to best. At number five, we have The Glass Cannon. It’s a trap for impatient players. You’ll get a few cheap kills, but one mistake and you’re back at Level 1. It’s overrated and frustrating. At number four is The Bullet Hose. It’s fun for absolute beginners, but the moment you face a player who knows how to keep their distance, you’re completely useless. It’s too one-dimensional. Number three is The Swarm. It’s a fantastic build for controlling space and dominating the Maze, but it lacks the burst damage to consistently take down high-tier tanks in FFA. It’s a solid, reliable pick, but not the absolute best. At number two, we have The Rusher. This is the most underrated build in the game. Everyone focuses on shooting, but ramming at max speed completely breaks the game’s physics and makes you an uncatchable nightmare. It’s pure chaos and incredibly effective. But the number one spot goes to The Sniper. It is simply the most mathematically broken strategy in Arras.io. 100% range and FOV means you control the entire map. Yes, it requires patience, but the ability to delete enemies before they even see you makes it the undisputed king.

Who Should Pick What

If you're a beginner
The Bullet Hose
It heavily forgives poor aim and lets you focus on learning map positioning and farming shapes safely. You won't get instantly sniped from across the map, making it the perfect learning tool.
If you love aggression
The Rusher
You get to completely ignore the traditional shooting meta and just use raw speed and health to run people over. It makes you an unpredictable terror who can escape any bad situation instantly.
If you play defensively
The Swarm
The autonomous projectiles create a perfect moving shield around your tank. This allows you to safely control narrow choke points in the Maze and deny area control without needing pinpoint accuracy.
If you want to dominate the leaderboard
The Sniper
Controlling the engagement distance with max range and FOV is the most consistent way to farm the Pentagon Nest. You can safely delete threats before they approach, securing the top leaderboard spot.

The Bottom Line

Look, I know everyone loves the flashy, high-speed chaos of the Rusher, but if you want the actual best build in Arras.io, you have to go with The Sniper. The data is undeniable: 100% range and field of view completely break the game’s balance. You aren't just playing the game; you're dictating it. You can farm the Pentagon Nest safely, control the Maze, and pick off anyone who dares to cross the open space. It requires patience and map awareness, but once you master the positioning, you are virtually untouchable. Stop rushing in and getting deleted. Max out your range, sit back, and watch the leaderboard climb.
